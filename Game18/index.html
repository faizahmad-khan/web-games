🃏 UNO

The Classic Card Game

📖 How to Play UNO

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to get rid of all your cards.

🃏 Card Types

  • Number Cards (0-9): Match by color or number.
  • Skip ⊘: Next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse ⇄: Reverses direction of play.
  • Draw Two +2: Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.
  • Wild 🌈: Play on any card. Choose the next color.
  • Wild Draw Four +4: Next player draws 4 cards. Choose the next color.

🕹️ How to Play

  • Click a card in your hand to play it (must match color, number, or symbol).
  • If you can't play, click the draw pile to draw a card.
  • When you have 1 card left, click the UNO button!
  • If you forget to call UNO, you draw 2 penalty cards.
