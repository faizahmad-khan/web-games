📖 How to Play

🏰

Defend Your Castle

Enemies march along the path toward your castle. Place towers along the route to stop them before they reach the gates!

🗼

Build Towers

Click empty grid cells to place towers. Each tower type has unique abilities — Archer towers attack fast, Mage towers deal area damage, Cannon towers hit hard!

⬆️

Upgrade & Evolve

Click existing towers to upgrade them. At level 3, towers can evolve into powerful specializations with unique abilities!

⚡

Hero Abilities

Your hero has 3 special abilities on cooldown. Use them wisely — a well-timed spell can turn the tide of battle!

💰

Economy & Strategy

Earn gold from defeated enemies. Balance between building new towers, upgrading existing ones, and saving for powerful abilities.
